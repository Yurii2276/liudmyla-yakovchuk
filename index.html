AUTHOR

Liudmyla Yakovchuk is a Ukrainian writer whose work explores memory, identity and the resilience of the human spirit. Born in the Korosten region of Ukraine, she has long been interested in the way family stories preserve what official history can overlook. Her fiction is rooted in real people, lived experience and historical truth, revealing the intimate choices, losses and loyalties that shape a nation across generations. In Remember Who You Are, she brings together different historical periods and human destinies to show how love, duty and memory can survive war, repression and displacement. Her aim is to make history feel human — not as a sequence of dates, but as something carried through ordinary lives.
